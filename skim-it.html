Skim It!

A quirky 2013 rock-skimming game by Flippin Pixels — preserved, plus two rebuilt versions.

What it is

Skim It! is a mobile game by Flippin Pixels, originally released around 2013 for iOS and Android. The goal is simple: skim rocks across the water and sink the little boats.

It was pulled from app stores years ago. Once a mobile game is delisted it effectively stops existing — you can't download it, and as phones move to newer operating systems, even people who still have a copy eventually lose the ability to run it. This project exists so that doesn't happen here.

How it plays

  • Levels are "Days" — each one split into four parts: Morning, Day, Night, and a Bonus Round
  • Bonus Rounds — sink ducks for coins, one coin per duck
  • Coins — spent on upgrades and revives
  • Glitches everywhere — some help you, some break things, some are just funny. They're part of the charm, not something the preserved build tries to hide

Versions

Skim It!+ Modded

Skim It! Remastered with a mod menu added on top — it carries all of Remastered's fixes, plus the menu. If you want the revived game and the extras, this is the one.

Compatibility

The archived original runs on:

  • iOS — 6, 8, 9 and 10
  • Android — 5 and 7
Known issue: on iOS 10 the music can glitch out and won't play. Everything else works.
Heads up: the original can be run through emulation, but outside of that it isn't modern-friendly. If you want to actually play it on a current device, grab Skim It!+ or Remastered instead.

Preservation notice

The archived original is provided for historical and educational preservation only.
