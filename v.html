Setting up with VTCS

  1. Make sure your Vector is running the VTCS firmware.
  2. Download and install the VTCS version of the Vector app (.apk). You may need to allow installs from unknown sources.
  3. In the app, create an account with your real email and a password, or sign in if you already have one. Your account keeps your Vectors and your weather and knowledge graph settings.
  4. Follow the app's instructions to finish activating your Vector. It's added to your account automatically.

Setting up the Vector App

  1. Go to the GitHub Releases page for Vector++ and download the latest .apk.
  2. Install the APK on your phone. (You may need to allow installs from unknown sources.)
  3. In the app, create a fake username and password. It never leaves your device and you'll never need it again.
  4. Follow the app's instructions to finish activating your Vector.
