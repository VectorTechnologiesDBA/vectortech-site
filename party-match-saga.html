Party Match Saga

A match-3 game for iOS and Android where you can swap in your own art, sounds, speech and ads.

What it is

Party Match Saga is a match-3 puzzle game built in Unity, currently v1.1, with versions for iOS and Android. It plays in portrait mode.

  • Levels with goals, special candies and combos
  • Tickets earned per level plus score
  • Shop — spend tickets on power-ups and extra lives or moves
  • Bonus rounds — Blackout, Chocolate, Glitch Out and Freeze Out, each ending with the bonus wheel
  • Settings in one screen behind the gear icon: ads, music, sound effects and speech toggles
  • Native text-to-speech announcer for any speech line you don't supply

Make your own asset pack

The whole gimmick: the game doesn't have its art and audio locked inside. It loads them at runtime from a zip file called Resources.zip. Build your own, hand it to the game, and everything you included replaces the default. Every asset is optional, so you can reskin one thing or all of it.

Quick start

  • 1. Make a folder named Resources, with a folder named PartyAssets inside it.
  • 2. Drop in the files you want to replace. Use the exact filenames listed below.
  • 3. Zip the Resources folder so the zip is named Resources.zip.
  • 4. Put Resources.zip in the right folder for your device (see Where it goes below), then open the game.
Rule of thumb: anything you leave out keeps its default. A speech line you leave out is read by text-to-speech instead.

Where it goes

  • iPhone — Files app > On My iPhone > Party Match Saga > Resources.zip
  • iPad — Files app > On My iPad > Party Match Saga > Resources.zip
  • Android — Downloads folder > PMS > Resources.zip

Folder layout

Resources.zip
└── Resources/
    └── PartyAssets/
        ├── Ads/                 (any video files, any names)
        ├── Speech/              (voice lines + loops)
        ├── Bubbles/             (bubble_<color>.png)
        ├── tile0.png ... tile4.png
        ├── music.mp3
        ├── ticket.png
        └── ...everything else, exact names

Ads

Put video files in PartyAssets/Ads/. These are the one place where filenames don't matter: use any names you like. The game plays an ad after each level, and players can turn ads off in settings.

Video formats: not fully tested yet. MP4 is recommended. Other video types might work, but we haven't tried them.

Board art

PNG images. Most come in five color variants numbered 0–4, and each has an alternate night version with _night added to the name (for example tile2_night.png).

  • tile0.png – tile4.png
  • square0.png – square4.png
  • bomb0.png – bomb4.png
  • stripeh0.png – stripeh4.png (horizontal stripes)
  • stripev0.png – stripev4.png (vertical stripes)
  • colorbomb.png (and colorbomb_night.png)
  • Bubbles/ — bubble_blue, bubble_green, bubble_orange, bubble_purple, bubble_red, bubble_teal, bubble_yellow (.png)

Bonus wheel and tickets

  • ticket.png — the ticket picture. It flies around and shows up as the small and big counter icon. A wide image, about 2:1, looks best.
  • wheel.png — the wheel disc. Square with transparent corners and 8 equal slices of 45°, slice 1 starting at 12 o'clock and running clockwise. Slice 5 (just past 6 o'clock, lower-left) is the jackpot. The game draws the numbers on top.
  • wheel_labeled.png — the same disc with the numbers already drawn into the art. If this is present, the game skips drawing its own numbers.
  • wheelpointer.png — the pointer at the top, drawn pointing down (tip at the bottom).
  • wheelbg.png — full-screen background behind the wheel.
  • Prize order, clockwise from 12 o'clock: 100, 250, 150, 500, 10000 (jackpot, slice 5), 300, 400, 750.

Sound effects and music

  • music — background music
  • swap, pop1 – pop6, bad, loose, win
  • special, square, bomb, stripe, colorbomb
  • tick — each slice clicking past on the wheel
  • wheelwin — a prize lands
  • jackpotsfx — the jackpot lands

Use WAV or MP3 files with these names.

Speech

Put voice recordings in PartyAssets/Speech/ as .mp3, .ogg or .wav, using these exact names. Any line you don't provide uses text-to-speech.

Gameplay

  • level_1 – level_30 — "Level N" at the start of each level
  • combo_2 – combo_10 — "Combo N"
  • radical — big pop
  • totally_awesome — combo 4 and up
  • boom — a special goes off
  • goal_reached — score goal hit
  • level_clear — level won
  • out_of_moves_try_again — level lost
  • no_moves_shuffling — the board shuffles
  • speech_on — when speech is turned on in settings

Your own cheers

  • extraline1, extraline2, extraline3… — number them 1, 2, 3 and so on. About 1 in 3 cheers gets swapped for a random one of these.

Bonus rounds and wheel

  • blackout_bonus, color_removed, blackout_complete, blackloop — Blackout
  • freeze_out, freeze_out_complete, freezeloop — Freeze Out
  • chocolate_time, chocolate_complete, chocoloop — Chocolate
  • glitch_out, system_restore (the board comes back after the outline is gone), glitch_complete, glitchloop — Glitch Out
  • bonus_wheel — the wheel starts spinning
  • jackpot — the wheel lands on the jackpot
Loops: the files ending in loop play on repeat during that bonus round at full volume while the song is muted. They only play if speech is on (and for Freeze Out and Chocolate, music too). If you don't supply glitchloop, that round is pure silence.

Tips

  • Start small: swap one thing, zip it, load it, and check it before building the whole pack.
  • Filenames are exact and are matched as written, so check spelling and numbering.
  • Keep your pack to things you made or have the rights to use.
